Whether you live in a neighborhood off Agate Road, run a business near the White City industrial park, or own property along Table Rock Road, Medford Junk Pros is right around the corner.

Medford Junk Pros, operated by Crovelle Junk Removal, proudly serves White City with the same fast, professional junk removal service we bring to all of Southern Oregon. No job is too big or too small — from a single appliance pickup to a full property cleanout, our crew is ready to help.
