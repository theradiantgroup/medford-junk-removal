Junk Removal in Jacksonville, Oregon

Fast, affordable, and eco-friendly junk hauling for Jacksonville homes and businesses

Jacksonville's Trusted Junk Removal Service

Jacksonville, Oregon is one of the best-preserved Gold Rush towns in the Pacific Northwest and a National Historic Landmark. Nestled in the Applegate Valley just west of Medford, Jacksonville is home to charming Victorian-era homes, the world-class Britt Festivals outdoor amphitheater, and a tight-knit community that takes great pride in its historic character. Keeping properties clean and free of clutter is especially important here, where curb appeal and historic integrity matter.

Medford Junk Pros, operated by Crovelle Junk Removal, proudly serves Jacksonville and the surrounding Applegate Valley with professional, reliable junk removal services. Whether you have an old shed full of equipment on a rural property, furniture to clear from a historic home on California Street, or post-renovation debris to haul away, our experienced crew is ready to help.

What We Remove in Jacksonville

Our team handles junk removal jobs of every size throughout Jacksonville. We remove old furniture, broken appliances, yard debris, construction waste, and just about anything else that is taking up space in your Jacksonville home, garage, barn, or business. We work with homeowners on rural Applegate Valley properties, landlords managing historic downtown rentals, and contractors working on restoration and renovation projects throughout the area.

Why Jacksonville Residents Choose Us

Jacksonville homeowners and property owners choose Medford Junk Pros because we treat every property with the care and respect it deserves. We understand that many Jacksonville homes are historic, and we take extra care not to damage landscaping, fencing, or structures as we haul items out. We also share the community's respect for the environment — we donate usable items to local charities, recycle materials wherever possible, and minimize landfill use.

Whether you are clearing out decades of accumulated belongings from a family home, cleaning up a barn or outbuilding on an Applegate Valley acreage, or hauling debris from a remodel near the historic downtown, our team arrives on time, works efficiently, and leaves your property spotless. We offer same-day and next-day service with upfront pricing and free estimates — no surprises.
