About Medford Junk Removal

Your local, trusted junk removal team in Southern Oregon

Who We Are

Medford Junk Removal is a service of Crovelle Junk Removal, proudly serving the Rogue Valley and all of Southern Oregon. We are a locally operated team that understands the unique needs of homeowners and businesses in Medford, Ashland, Central Point, Grants Pass, and the surrounding communities. When you call us, you are working with people who live and work right here in Southern Oregon.

We started with a simple goal: provide honest, affordable junk removal that people can count on. Too many junk removal companies surprise customers with hidden fees, show up late, or leave a mess behind. We do things differently. Every job begins with a free, no-obligation estimate so you know exactly what to expect. Our crew shows up on time, works efficiently, and leaves your property clean and clutter-free.

Our Mission

Our mission is straightforward: help Southern Oregon residents and businesses reclaim their spaces by providing reliable, affordable, and eco-friendly junk removal services. We believe that getting rid of clutter should be simple and stress-free, and we work hard to make that a reality for every customer.

We are committed to keeping Southern Oregon beautiful. That means going the extra mile to dispose of your junk responsibly. We donate usable items to local charities throughout the Rogue Valley, recycle materials whenever possible, and only send items to the landfill as an absolute last resort. Our eco-friendly approach means you can feel good about clearing out your space.

What Sets Us Apart

Locally Operated

We are not a national franchise. We are a local Southern Oregon team that knows the Rogue Valley and cares about this community. When you call us, you are supporting a local business.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees, no bait-and-switch quotes. We provide a free estimate upfront so you know exactly what your junk removal will cost before we start.

Eco-Friendly Disposal

We donate, recycle, and repurpose whenever we can. Landfill is always our last resort. We are committed to keeping Southern Oregon clean and green.

Reliable & On Time

We show up when we say we will. Same-day and next-day service available throughout the Rogue Valley, seven days a week.

Insured & Professional

Our crew is fully insured and experienced. We treat your property with respect and handle every job with care and professionalism.

Customer Focused

Your satisfaction is our priority. We are not happy until you are happy, and we go out of our way to earn your trust and your referral.

Our Service Area

We serve homes and businesses throughout Southern Oregon, including Medford, Ashland, Central Point, Grants Pass, Jacksonville, Phoenix, Talent, Eagle Point, White City, Rogue River, Gold Hill, Shady Cove, and surrounding areas in the Rogue Valley and Josephine County. If you are in Southern Oregon and need junk removed, chances are we can help.
