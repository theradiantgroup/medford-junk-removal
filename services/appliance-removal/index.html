Safe & Responsible Appliance Removal in Medford, OR

Old appliances are some of the most difficult items to get rid of on your own. Refrigerators, washers, and dryers are extremely heavy, often contain hazardous materials like refrigerants, and most curbside pickup services will not take them. If you have old or broken appliances sitting in your Medford home, garage, or property, Medford Junk Pros is here to help. We specialize in the safe removal and proper disposal of all types of household and commercial appliances.

Our trained crew knows how to handle large, heavy appliances without damaging your floors, walls, or doorways. Whether your old refrigerator is in a tight kitchen space or a broken washer is sitting in the basement, we have the equipment and experience to remove it safely. We handle refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ovens, ranges, dishwashers, microwaves, water heaters, air conditioning units, and window AC units. No appliance is too big or too awkward for our team.

Proper appliance disposal is important for the environment. Many appliances contain refrigerants, oils, and metals that require special handling under EPA regulations. At Medford Junk Pros, we ensure that every appliance we remove is disposed of in compliance with local and federal environmental guidelines. We work with certified recycling facilities in Southern Oregon to recover metals and components, keeping as much material as possible out of the landfill.

Do not let old appliances take up valuable space in your home or become a safety hazard. Our appliance removal service is available seven days a week throughout Medford, Ashland, Central Point, Grants Pass, and the entire Rogue Valley. We offer competitive pricing with free, upfront estimates so there are never any surprises. Call us today at 971-371-1472 and let us take those old appliances off your hands.
