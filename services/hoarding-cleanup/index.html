Compassionate Hoarding Cleanup Services in Medford, OR

Hoarding is a deeply personal and often emotionally complex situation. Whether you are helping a family member reclaim their home, preparing a property for sale, or taking the courageous first step for yourself, Medford Junk Pros approaches every hoarding cleanup with patience, respect, and zero judgment. Our team understands that behind every accumulation of belongings is a person and a story, and we treat every client and every property with the dignity and care they deserve.

Our hoarding cleanup process is methodical and works at your pace. We never rush you or pressure you to let go of items that matter. We typically work room by room, clearing pathways first for safety, then systematically removing items you've identified for disposal. We sort as we go — separating items for donation, recycling, and disposal — and we communicate clearly throughout the process so you always know what's happening and what's going where. For larger hoarding situations, we can work over multiple days to minimize overwhelm.

Hoarding situations in Medford and throughout the Rogue Valley range widely in scale, from homes with moderate accumulation in a few rooms to properties that haven't been fully accessible in years. Regardless of the level, we have the crew size, equipment, and experience to handle the job safely. We can also coordinate with property managers, real estate agents, or family members who may be assisting with the cleanup if needed.

All calls and visits are completely confidential. We arrive in unmarked trucks if requested, and we never discuss client situations with anyone outside the project. If you're ready to take the first step, call us at 971-317-1472 for a free, private, no-obligation estimate. We're here to help, not to judge.
