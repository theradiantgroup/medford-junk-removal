Professional Garage Cleanout Services in Medford, OR

For most Medford homeowners, the garage has become the catch-all room — the place where old sports equipment, broken tools, boxes from three moves ago, and everything else without a home ends up piling up over the years. If your garage has reached the point where you can barely get your car in, or you've simply stopped opening the door to avoid the chaos, Medford Junk Pros is here to help. We provide fast, thorough garage cleanout services that get your space back in one visit.

Our garage cleanout crew handles every type of accumulated junk. We remove old tools and hardware, broken lawn equipment, outdated electronics, piles of cardboard boxes, old paint cans and chemicals (we can advise on proper hazardous disposal), rusted bicycles, holiday decorations you'll never use again, and anything else taking up space. No load is too big or too small — whether your garage is mildly cluttered or hasn't seen a car in a decade, we've handled it all throughout the Rogue Valley.

We know that garages often accumulate items with sentimental or uncertain value, which is why we work at your direction. You decide what stays and what goes. Our crew does the heavy lifting — sorting, carrying, and loading everything you want removed. We make multiple passes through if needed to ensure nothing is accidentally discarded that you want to keep. Once the junk is out, we do a final sweep so you're left with a clean, empty space ready to actually use.

After the cleanout, we sort everything responsibly. Usable tools, equipment, and household goods are donated to local Southern Oregon charities whenever possible. Recyclable materials go to the appropriate facilities. Only what cannot be donated or recycled goes to the landfill.
