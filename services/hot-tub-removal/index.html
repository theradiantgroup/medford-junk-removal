Hot Tub & Spa Removal Services in Medford, OR

Old hot tubs are one of the most difficult items to remove from a property. They're massive, heavy, often hardwired or plumbed into the home, and nearly impossible to move without the right equipment and experience. Whether your hot tub stopped working years ago, you're selling your Medford home and the buyers don't want it, or you simply want your backyard space back, Medford Junk Pros provides complete hot tub removal from start to finish.

Our hot tub removal process covers everything. We start by draining the tub completely, then safely disconnect any electrical connections (we recommend confirming power is off at the breaker before our arrival). Next, our crew dismantles the hot tub on-site — cutting it into manageable sections to get it out of tight backyard spaces, through gates, or around obstacles. We then haul away every piece: the shell, the frame, the insulation panels, and any attached components. When we're done, you won't be able to tell the hot tub was ever there.

Hot tub removal in Southern Oregon is a specialty service because the terrain and tight backyard layouts in many Medford, Ashland, and Grants Pass properties make access a real challenge. Our team has the tools and experience to handle narrow side yards, fenced properties, deck-mounted tubs, and sunken installations. No matter where your hot tub is located, we'll get it out cleanly and safely.

We recycle and responsibly dispose of all hot tub materials wherever possible — fiberglass shells, metal frames, and pumps can often be broken down and recycled rather than dumped. Call us at 971-371-1472 for a free estimate on hot tub removal anywhere in the Medford area or greater Rogue Valley.
