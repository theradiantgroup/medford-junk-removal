Construction Debris Removal for Medford Renovations & Remodels

Home renovations and remodeling projects in Medford can transform your living space, but they always generate a significant amount of waste. Piles of drywall, old lumber, broken tile, torn-out carpet, and other construction materials can quickly overwhelm your property and slow down your project. Medford Junk Pros provides fast, efficient construction debris removal so you can keep your job site clean and your renovation on schedule.

We work with homeowners, contractors, and property managers throughout the Rogue Valley to provide reliable construction waste hauling. Whether you are doing a kitchen remodel, bathroom renovation, roof replacement, or full home demolition, we have the trucks and manpower to handle debris of any volume. Our crew removes drywall and sheetrock, framing lumber and plywood, ceramic and porcelain tile, old carpet and padding, concrete and masonry, roofing shingles and underlayment, insulation, cabinetry, and all other renovation waste materials.

Unlike renting a dumpster that takes up your driveway for days or weeks, our crew provides on-demand debris removal that clears your site quickly. We can schedule regular pickups throughout the duration of your project or provide a single large-scale cleanup when the work is complete. This flexibility makes us an ideal partner for contractors who need to keep their Medford job sites tidy and compliant with local regulations.

We take responsible disposal seriously. All construction materials are sorted at our facility, with recyclable metals, wood, and concrete diverted from the landfill whenever possible. Our goal is to minimize the environmental impact of every construction project we service in Southern Oregon.
