Professional Furniture Removal Services in Medford, OR

Whether you are upgrading your living room, downsizing, or clearing out a rental property, getting rid of old furniture can be a real challenge. Large items like couches, sofas, and mattresses are heavy, awkward to move, and impossible to fit in a standard vehicle. That is where Medford Junk Pros comes in. We provide fast, reliable furniture removal throughout Medford and the greater Rogue Valley, so you do not have to struggle with hauling bulky items yourself.

Our experienced crew handles all types of household and office furniture. From oversized sectional sofas and worn-out recliners to outdated dressers, dining tables, and bulky entertainment centers, there is no piece of furniture we cannot remove. We also take bed frames, box springs, mattresses, bookshelves, desks, office chairs, and end tables. If it is taking up space in your home and you want it gone, we will take care of it quickly and professionally.

At Medford Junk Pros, we are committed to responsible disposal. When your furniture is still in usable condition, we make every effort to donate it to local charities and organizations in Southern Oregon. Items that cannot be donated are recycled whenever possible, and we only send materials to the landfill as a last resort. Our eco-friendly approach means you can feel good knowing your old furniture is being handled responsibly.

We understand that your time is valuable, which is why we offer same-day and next-day furniture removal in Medford and surrounding areas including Ashland, Central Point, and Grants Pass. Our pricing is transparent and upfront with no hidden fees. Just give us a call at 971-317-1472 for a free, no-obligation estimate, and we will have your unwanted furniture hauled away before you know it.
