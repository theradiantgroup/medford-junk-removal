Yard Waste & Green Debris Removal in Medford, OR

Southern Oregon's beautiful landscape means Medford homeowners are constantly maintaining yards, gardens, and outdoor spaces. Whether you have just finished a major landscaping project, trimmed back overgrown trees, or are dealing with the aftermath of one of the Rogue Valley's seasonal storms, yard waste can pile up fast. Medford Junk Pros offers reliable yard waste removal services that take the burden off your shoulders and leave your property looking clean and well-maintained.

Our team handles all types of yard waste and green debris. We remove fallen branches and tree limbs, old stumps and root balls, piles of leaves and grass clippings, excess soil and dirt, sod and turf, hedge trimmings, bush clippings, and all forms of landscaping debris. If you have had trees removed or trimmed by an arborist who left the debris behind, we can clear everything quickly and affordably. No pile is too large or too scattered for our crew to handle.

Storm season in the Rogue Valley can leave Medford properties covered with fallen limbs, scattered debris, and damaged landscaping materials. When storms hit, our team is ready to provide fast cleanup services so you can get your property back to normal. We understand the urgency that comes with storm damage, which is why we offer priority scheduling for storm cleanup throughout Medford, Ashland, Central Point, Grants Pass, and surrounding areas.

All yard waste we collect is taken to appropriate green waste facilities in Southern Oregon where it can be composted or processed responsibly. We never mix green waste with general landfill materials when it can be diverted. Our commitment to eco-friendly disposal means your yard waste is handled in the most environmentally conscious way possible.
